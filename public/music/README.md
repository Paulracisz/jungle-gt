# Music

With an empty `playlist.json` the game plays its built-in **procedural jungle**
(synthesised live, nothing to license). To use real tracks instead:

1. Copy your `.mp3` / `.ogg` files into this folder.
2. Copy `playlist.example.json` over `playlist.json` and list each track.
3. Redeploy. `N` skips to the next track in game; `M` mutes.

`credit` is shown in the HUD, which is how you meet CC-BY attribution.

## Licensing, since GitHub Pages republishes the files

Anything in `public/` is served to every visitor and sits in your public repo.
Prefer tracks licensed **CC0** or **CC BY**, and keep the licence text/URL
somewhere in the repo. Check each track's own page; licences are per track,
not per site. Sites that host openly licensed jungle / drum & bass:

- Free Music Archive (filter by licence)
- ccMixter
- Bandcamp artists who publish under Creative Commons
- Internet Archive netlabels
- Pixabay Music and Incompetech: free to use, but read their terms about
  redistribution before committing the audio files to a public repo.
